# Casework redesign — development edition

This is the dedicated detective-game rebuild. It is not the published main edition and is not yet approved as a complete game. The published game, earlier editions and saves remain separate.

The authored foundation and progression live in src/content.js. Private chronology and character knowledge are in docs/rebuild/private and never enter the playable build. Original apartment images come from a single project-owned Blender scene in tools/rebuild. Finished audio is generated locally; models do not ship.

Use the allowlisted build/preview workflow rather than publishing the source repository. The new save key is lastarchive.casework.v1. Do not overwrite earlier edition keys or migrate their story decisions into this new case.
