# Blind-playtesting protocol — spoiler-free

The author/developer has read the complete case and cannot supply a blind first-player verdict. No blind-player results are claimed. Keep solutions, source code, internal notes, automated walkthroughs and the previous edition away from participants testing the revised story.

## Sessions

Recruit three to five independent first-time players, ideally including an experienced mystery player and a player less familiar with investigative interfaces. Include desktop, physical iPhone Safari and Android Chrome sessions. Use volunteers or people already available; no paid testing subscription is required.

Run the packaged revised edition from a clean save. Do not provide coaching, internal solutions or previews of later scenes. Permit built-in hints, notes, transcript-only play and ordinary breaks. Ask players to voice current hypotheses at chapter boundaries; do not confirm or deny them. Record sensitive observations privately with permission, keeping all reports intended for the owner spoiler-free.

## Measurements

Record start/finish, pauses, reading/listening, reasoning, navigation/friction, hints, retries and interruptions separately. Use active time, not automated execution time or a file/word-count estimate. Log where a participant first reaches a supported conclusion, how confident they are and which sources they cite. Distinguish a fair early inference from a guess.

At each chapter ask:

- What question are you trying to answer, and what could you try next?
- Which record do you currently trust most, and why?
- Is any control or required source difficult to discover?
- Did a scene change how you felt about a character, rather than merely adding information?
- Was any passage repetitive, too explicit, implausible or emotionally hurried?

After the ending, collect private ratings for engagement, emotional investment, distinct voices, fairness, surprise in retrospect and satisfaction with closure. Ask whether the earlier material supports later understanding. Log unresolved questions privately; never include their actual content in a spoiler-free owner report.

## Required variants

Across sessions exercise both interview approaches, both listening/privacy approaches, all public-correction options and at least one no-confrontation route. Include muted/transcript-only and save/reload sessions. Test offline download, airplane-mode reload, interrupted playback and actual touch/pinch/keyboard use on physical devices.

## Revision gates

Do not pad the game to achieve 60–90+ minutes. If active play is short, improve meaningful reasoning and scenes; if it is long through friction, repair navigation and presentation. A supported account must be reachable without a confession, hearing audio, or surrendering an unnecessary privacy boundary. Resolve any misleading mandatory deduction, accidental spoiler, unacknowledged contradiction or inaccessible control before proposing release.

If emotional development, surprise or the final act does not work, return to the text and causal model before producing more assets. The owner's architectural approval is not release approval.
