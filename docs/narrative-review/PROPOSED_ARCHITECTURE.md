# THE LAST ARCHIVE — proposed narrative architecture

Spoiler-free approval overview · 16 November 2026

**Status: architecture complete; rewrite not implemented.** The nine-chapter dramatic outline, objective chronology, character knowledge matrix, clue/revelation map, misleading-lead resolutions and decision structure are maintained in private planning files outside the repository and game build. This document contains no culprit, hidden identity, secret relationship, puzzle answer, actual reversal or ending explanation.

## Creative direction

The revised game centres on the tension between knowing someone and deciding what their life should mean. The archive becomes an emotionally contested part of the story. The investigator has a credible commission, defined access and understandable reasons to keep looking. New sources have identifiable custodians and reasons to become available.

The existing cast and useful locations remain the foundation, but major actions, relationships, the source's role and the final dramatic construction have been reconsidered. An existing plot decision is not protected simply because it already has assets or a working puzzle.

The intended experience is a psychological mystery with human stakes: genuine affection alongside resentment, practical kindness alongside conflicting obligations, and grief that does not make anyone simple or interchangeable. Several ordinary moments will remain ordinary even after the resolution. Emotional investment needs something real to lose.

## What changes substantially

- **Character development:** each central person has a distinct desire, fear, communication style, boundary and relationship arc. Interviews react to established disclosures rather than returning mechanically to an earlier denial. Iris has consequential choices, flaws and relationships throughout the archive.
- **Dramatic reversals:** the private outline contains three principal reversals with different functions. They change the understanding of a person, the interpretation of the available record, and the meaning of responsibility. This is a working structure, not a quota; a reversal may be consolidated if that strengthens the emotional arc. Their details, placement and connections remain private.
- **Misdirection:** every substantial alternative explanation has persuasive support, a reason its source might be misleading, independent ways to test it and an explicit resolution. Resolving a suspicion does not erase the person's other responsibilities.
- **Retrospective meaning:** selected conversations, images and recordings have planned later readings. Those readings use information the player could inspect beforehand; they do not depend on an indispensable new fact delivered in the final speech.
- **Atmosphere:** unease comes from familiar rooms, interrupted ordinary exchanges, intimate objects and inconsistencies between public accounts and private records. Sound has an authored context and purpose. Quiet, warmth and humour provide contrast and emotional credibility.
- **Final act:** the final three chapters provide space to reconstruct, challenge and confront the account, then experience reactions and aftermath. Important questions and consequential side threads receive closure.

## All nine chapters: player experience

The labels below describe design functions, not the proposed story titles or the timing of its revelations.

| Chapter | Investigation focus | Dramatic purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish the task, examine the delivered material and form a first supported question. | Introduce people through lived details and give the player a credible role. |
| 2 | Compare permissions, documents and accounts; begin subject dossiers. | Develop relationships and make the cost of asking questions understandable. |
| 3 | Cross-check movement records, observations and physical details. | Give characters' actions context beyond suspicion alone. |
| 4 | Examine original materials and compare available versions. | Develop competing values and the consequences of earlier choices. |
| 5 | Work with source media and contextual corroboration. | Deepen the player's relationship with the people supplying information. |
| 6 | Compare testimony, records and uncertainty across a common timeline. | Resolve misleading leads through meaningful human consequences. |
| 7 | Test the most consequential claims against independent support. | Give difficult discoveries space to affect the characters. |
| 8 | Construct a defensible account and challenge its strongest alternative. | Let the player reach understanding before the conclusion confirms it. |
| 9 | Choose an appropriate approach to confrontation or referral; experience responses and aftermath. | Deliver factual closure and an emotional ending with room to land. |

Each chapter has specified scenes, available sources, player actions, deductions, exit conditions and emotional movement in the private outline. They are not simply nine batches of newly unlocked files.

## Investigative gameplay

Retain and rebuild the existing activities where they serve the revised story:

- Photographic inspection and comparison of available versions.
- Physical document examination, front/reverse information and properly sourced alternate-light captures.
- Ridge and physical-trace comparisons with clear limits on what they establish.
- Audio investigation with equivalent transcripts, visual traces and accessible controls.
- A justified decoding/indexing activity whose result is applied to an actual investigative question.
- Surveillance calibration, interval marking, maps and explicit travel constraints.
- Timelines separating observations, testimony and estimates.
- Evidence-based interviews, subject notes and a deduction board with a list-based alternative.
- Final case construction that requires support, handles reasonable alternatives and distinguishes findings from overclaims.

An activity is retained because its result matters. Repetitive exercises may be consolidated. Arbitrary code hunts or hidden mechanical devices will not be added where the story has no credible use for them; restoring and examining source material provides the corresponding engagement.

## Decisions and consequences

The architecture includes choices about interview approach, boundaries around intimate material, and responsibility for how findings are communicated. These affect cooperation, scenes, trust, reputational consequences and the aftermath.

The underlying facts stay consistent. A privacy-respecting choice cannot remove a necessary clue. Declining audio or a personal confrontation cannot prevent factual closure. No mandatory conclusion depends on obtaining an exhaustive confession, and the game does not reward destroying inconvenient evidence.

## Internal consistency and fairness

The private planning package contains:

- A dated chronology covering the relevant history, the incident and investigation, with travel intervals and clock conventions.
- A nine-chapter knowledge matrix for the central characters and investigator, separating knowledge, mistaken belief, withheld information and acknowledgment.
- A clue map recording availability, origin, supported claim, inference limit and intended later significance.
- A revelation map with earlier setup and independently originated supporting routes before confirmation.
- A resolution ledger for misleading leads and a decision-equivalence policy for required evidence.
- Automated architecture checks for dates, dependencies, travel, knowledge and premature disclosure, alongside an editorial causal stress test.

These checks validate the plan's consistency. They do not establish that unwritten scenes already work, that the emotional impact is proven, or that a playable first investigation lasts 60–90+ minutes. Those require the completed text and blind playtests.

## Images, language and free production

The supplied images inform material texture and composition: annotated records, papers with custody history, personal photographs, maps, quiet surveillance views and notebooks. New materials will be original fictional English assets appropriate to this story.

The full game remains English, including documents, visible image lettering, dialogue, recordings and captions. After the revised scripts pass their internal editorial and consistency review, affected speech will be generated locally with free tooling. Only finished audio will ship. Existing free-only production and hosting constraints remain in force.

## Approval boundary

Approval of this architecture direction would authorise the full narrative rewrite and its integration. Before final audiovisual production, the complete revised text should pass its continuity and proof review. Rewritten case progress must be treated as a new narrative edition while preserving the old save separately.

The final-third pacing allocation and a 60–90+ minute meaningful first investigation are design targets, not measured claims. Release readiness requires blind-player evidence for fairness, emotional impact and pacing, plus the remaining technical/device checks from the paused gameplay audit.

**Nothing has been rewritten in the running game, merged or deployed. Await architectural approval before implementation.**
