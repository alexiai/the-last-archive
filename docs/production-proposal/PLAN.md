# THE LAST ARCHIVE — expanded production plan

**Approval gate:** The user requested analysis and a concrete plan before implementation. This is a proposal, not a claim that the features below are already built. This branch adds only the proposal, licences and neutral English voice auditions. No merge, deployment or narrative implementation is authorized by this document alone.

## 1. Creative and language contract

Everything the player reads or hears will be English: UI, dialogue, clues, documents, image lettering, subtitles, recordings, notifications and credits. Established character and place names remain proper nouns; address prefixes, directions, currency labels and abbreviations will be expressed understandably in English. Text embedded in generated or composited imagery receives its own proofread. Voices use English pronunciation direction; no Romanian dialogue or new untranslated content is introduced.

The visual references suggest a tactile investigation: physical evidence, layered archival sources, imperfect records, marked plans, surveillance and objects that reward examination. They are not story canon and do not provide asset reuse rights. Create original fictional English materials with the established cast and locations. Treat occult material as something that requires a grounded narrative purpose rather than a shortcut to unexplained events. Major events and revelation details remain out of user-facing documents.

The archive-desk identity remains the foundation. Work should expand the investigation and its media, not begin another general visual redesign.

## 2. Narrative development before expansion

The current story has a usable nine-chapter spine and several recontextualizing discoveries. Its weaknesses are compression, short conversations, thin independent corroboration, some timing language, and abrupt transitions from an account to a conclusion. Legacy development notes also need reconciliation against the final playable canon.

Prepare a private continuity and causality matrix covering:

- What objectively occurred, what each person knows, what they believe, and what they tell the player.
- Each important deduction, its available supporting sources, contradictory sources, timing and strength.
- Every revelation's earlier setup and later consequences. Prefer two independent supporting routes for mandatory deductions.
- Innocent explanations for misleading records; red herrings must resolve rather than simply disappear.
- Dates, travel durations, device ownership, clock offsets, provenance and document access.
- Character-specific communication habits and emotional progression, including meaningful ordinary interactions.
- A final resolution that addresses central questions and consequential side threads without an explanatory information dump.

Preserve the useful chapter spine, existing evidence IDs, existing solved-puzzle meanings and save continuity. Add or restructure scenes where the matrix reveals weak motivation or unsupported inference. Do not write new scenes solely to meet a message count. Future user reports discuss consistency, pacing and fairness rather than what happens.

**Deliverable:** an internally coherent revised canon and clue map; a spoiler-free summary of its structural improvements. The new material must be authored before generating its final media.

## 3. Repair the demonstrated defects first

- Start ordering puzzles in a deterministic, genuinely unsolved order; keep their answers and validators.
- Apply both chapter and required-flag gates to recordings and their evidence actions.
- Restore a visible, confirmed restart action on desktop and mobile.
- Save unfinished puzzle input, including final case-file selections, with validation and recovery.
- Surface consequential decisions as current tasks rather than burying them beneath the board list.
- Make important deductions part of chapter reasoning, with an accessible worksheet/list route equivalent to the canvas.
- Resolve desktop dock/notebook overlap and fixed positioning inside transformed paper views.
- Add evidence and interview-presentation filters; preserve scroll, context and unsent notes.
- Reconcile inconsistent temporal wording against the approved private continuity matrix.

Completed legacy saves remain valid. Added requirements need a migration policy: retain completed work, map old flags to equivalent milestones, and expose newly available material without trapping existing players or erasing their notes. Use fixtures from each chapter and completed saves to verify this.

## 4. Mechanic and media plan

Each feature must change what the player can infer or do. An image, waveform or button is not considered the mechanic itself.

| Reference idea | Planned interaction | Fairness and production boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Evidence board and red threads | Group records, make and annotate deductions, present relevant relationships at investigation checkpoints | Confirmed and tentative relationships remain distinct; avoid forcing every possible connection. Canvas and list routes must produce equivalent progress. |
| Fingerprints | Align a scene sample with comparison samples and mark a small set of relevant ridge features | Use original synthetic print graphics. A correspondence can support contact, not automatically establish time or intent. Provide a text-accessible comparison route. |
| Shoeprints and physical traces | Compare scale, tread features, orientation and partial damage | Do not equate a common tread with unique identification. Explain the inference limit in feedback. |
| Medical/legal reports | Inspect original English reports, distinguish measured findings from estimated windows, compare them with other records | Authored findings must agree with the case. Avoid arbitrary medical precision or conclusions unsupported by the document. |
| Floor plans and maps | Compare plans, access points and independently timestamped movements; annotate routes and discrepancies | Original fictional plans for established locations. Do not promise a particular spatial revelation in the spoiler-free plan. |
| UV/infrared inspection | Switch between properly sourced visible-light and forensic capture channels; inspect and collect the revealed material | A physical object's alternate capture must be available through a credible source. A screen is not a magical UV scanner of arbitrary photographs. |
| Letters, clippings and diaries | Examine fronts/reverses, compare versions and dates, connect public accounts with private records | Use original English fictional material. A photograph's reverse applies to a physical print, not every digital image. |
| Ciphers, Morse and multi-step locks | Identify a method from available evidence, extract information, then apply it to a justified lock or deduction | Clue the method and key. Offer audio/visual equivalence, bounded hints and retries; never depend on an unexplained arbitrary code. |
| CCTV and surveillance | Scrub short clips, mark a relevant interval, compare cameras and check the clock/source | Produce a motion-bearing prototype first. Use original authored motion or explicitly licensed footage. Do not call a static image behind controls a video. Silence is valid when a source has no microphone. |
| Suspect case files | Keep per-person alibis, statements, contradictions, links and player annotations | Separate verified records from testimony and player theories. Save annotations automatically. |
| Browser and location search | Search an authored local index of people, addresses, places and recovered pages; cross-link sources | No dependency on live internet search. Wrong queries receive useful, non-revealing guidance rather than a hidden exact-string trap. |

Prototype the technically riskiest pieces—motion footage, forensic comparison, channel inspection and expressive speech—before generating their complete asset sets. If a prototype does not meet quality, revise its production method rather than label an imitation as complete.

## 5. English audio production

### Local generation

Use Kokoro locally during development, with approved English stock voices, consistent character casting and a pronunciation dictionary. Retain dry WAV masters and render MP3 gameplay copies, with WAV fallbacks only where needed. The browser plays files; it does not load the model or make generation requests.

Current study: four English voices and two different processing treatments exist. All six masters pass the word-level ASR check; they still require listening assessment. Kokoro is fast and intelligible here, but its limited expressive control means punctuation and speed are not substitutes for acting. Regenerate and edit weak takes; do not disguise them with noise. Do not clone a real actor or voice from the reference images.

### Recording coverage

Create a scene-by-scene recording manifest for:

- Existing voicemail and voice-note entries, preserving their important words and evidentiary meaning.
- Incoming and unknown calls with answer, decline and replay/recovery behavior.
- Authored two-sided telephone conversations where appropriate.
- Selected interviews or recorded statements with subtitles and usable transcripts.
- Surveillance/archival audio only when the source plausibly records sound.
- Other justified narrative recordings approved in the revised continuity map.

Different people must remain distinguishable across all these media. Unidentified voices need a coherent masking policy that does not accidentally disclose hidden identities through credits, filenames, audition notes or exposed casting maps.

### Editing and sound

Make a performance pass before processing. Use channel-specific treatments: telephone bandwidth and line noise; voicemail compression and beeps; quiet close voice notes; spatial room reflections; distant surveillance microphones; archival flutter or dropouts only for sources that justify them. Avoid a universal distortion filter. Keep important clue-bearing speech intelligible and provide exact English subtitles, speaker labels appropriate to the player's knowledge, and relevant non-speech captions.

**Acceptance:** every recording is a non-empty, decodable local asset, correctly mapped, synchronized with its transcript, replayable, controllable and free of accidental clipping. Listen on headphones and phone speakers; ASR is a supplementary check, not an acting-quality certificate.

## 6. Scripted events, music and sound design

Build a data-driven event director keyed to story flags, discoveries and chapter state. Persist one-shot event completion. It should schedule meaningful calls, notifications and unsettling events at authored moments, allow deferred/declined calls to be recovered, and pause or queue interruptions around fragile interactions. Avoid random events whose only purpose is to startle.

Use original project-authored local assets for ringing, message cues, discovery cues, electrical/room/urban ambience, transitions, suspense stems and isolated unsettling sounds. Produce and review music as an actual composition, not an endlessly loud drone. Record silence deliberately in the score map. Any outside asset needs a compatible source licence and attribution before inclusion.

Audio architecture:

- Initialize/resume the audio context from Start, Enable sound, answer-call or play gestures. Catch rejected playback and offer an explicit retry.
- Separate master, dialogue, effects and music volume; persist settings and expose mute and reduced-fright options.
- Limit to one foreground narrative voice. Queue or pause other speech. Duck music/ambience during dialogue and restore levels smoothly.
- Pause appropriately on tab hiding, navigation and interrupted mobile audio sessions. Handle ended, error and decode failures.
- Add ringing and optional `navigator.vibrate` with graceful unsupported-device fallback. Vibration is never required for a clue.
- Provide pause/replay/seek and accessible captions. Plot progress cannot depend on hearing or a working vibration motor.

**Acceptance:** no uncontrolled overlap, no involuntary autoplay, no repeated one-shot scares after reload, and no lost story content if a call is declined or audio fails.

## 7. Static and offline delivery

Keep all approved assets inside the static build using repository-relative paths. Add a versioned service worker and asset manifest with an explicit complete-archive download. Mark the archive offline-ready only after required files have been successfully cached and verified. Previously visited screens alone do not establish offline completeness.

Test fresh install, interrupted downloads, updates, storage denial/quota failure, stale caches and offline reload. Cache updates must not erase local saves or notes. The user can continue in online/static mode when caching is unavailable, with an honest status. Models, Python, FFmpeg and neural inference remain development tools, not shipped dependencies.

## 8. Pacing and quality gates

Target a measured 60–90+ minute first investigation through varied reasoning, listening, observation, comparison and confrontation. No duration claim until first-time human tests exist. Separate time spent reasoning from time lost scrolling, searching for controls or replaying unclear speech.

Retain existing puzzles where fair; introduce integrated multi-step tasks only where their evidence is available and their consequences advance the case. Revisit earlier sources with changed understanding. Add contextual conversations and media because they establish relationships, corroborate findings or change interpretation, not to inflate word counts.

Run:

- Existing 275 gameplay checks and 125 browser checks as a baseline, updating tests when intentional approved behavior changes.
- New gating, ordering-randomization, draft-save, decision, board-reasoning and old-save migration tests.
- All nine chapters and every branch, both normal and deliberately poor interview sequences, through UI actions as well as engine reachability.
- Audio asset existence/decoding, source/transcript mapping, mixing, rejected autoplay, pause/replay, volume and offline tests.
- Mobile layout/gesture/keyboard checks at 320px, 390px and larger sizes; actual iOS Safari/Android checks when physical devices are available.
- A clean repository-subdirectory build and a pre-deployment test gate. No merge or deployment occurs during this work.

## 9. Implementation sequence after approval

1. **Canon and production specification:** private continuity matrix, clue/puzzle map, English terminology and pronunciation rules, spoiler-free acceptance checklist.
2. **Repair and save compatibility:** demonstrated defects, input persistence, decision discoverability, backward-save fixtures and reasoning checkpoints.
3. **Risk prototypes and cast:** evaluate voice auditions; prototype one call, one motion-bearing surveillance extract, one forensic comparison and one channel-inspection task. Continue production only with a credible implementation method.
4. **Full narrative/media integration:** expanded meaningful conversations, reports, letters, local browser index, location work, per-subject case files and approved investigation tasks across the nine-chapter progression.
5. **Complete audio and event production:** consistent English performances, exact subtitles, scene-specific processing, original score/effects and event timing, mixers and accessibility settings.
6. **Offline packaging and regression:** service worker, manifest, complete caching, licence/asset ledger, full progression/branch/mobile checks and human playtest findings.

The stages are work packages within one approved scope, not a plan to stop after the first screen. Further reports will state what actually exists, what has been tested and what remains unverified. No spoiler-bearing canon or casting details will appear in the user-facing report.
