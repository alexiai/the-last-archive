## Nine chapter architecture and causal discoveries
1. THE ROOM LEFT BEHIND: cinematic assignment; known facts only; enter the consistent apartment; inspect table, shelf, doorway; recover the authorised phone copy. Compare current room with an older personal photo. Finding establishes a change, not who changed it or when. Unlock voluntary contact and external records requests.
2. A LIFE IN THE DEVICE: ordinary authored conversations, calendar and receipts; independently reconstruct the last known journey. Phone photo and reminder establish that Iris prepared the room before the death. Earlier furniture observation changes meaning; a missing shelf object becomes the useful difference. Suspect conversations remain humane.
3. THE MINUTES BETWEEN: actual hotel footage/stills, recorder calibration, independent transport/receiving records; construct a feasible interval. Footprints are compatible classes; wheel tracks belong to the case, not a second person. Resolve Luca's apparent roster contradiction. Request archive custody sources.
4. THE KIND VERSION: compare retained messages, source versions and approvals; distinguish family listening from public description. Iris participated in earlier edits. Mara's secret becomes emotionally comprehensible without erasing her responsibility. Resolve the presumed exposure/blackmail purpose of the packet using its independent duplicate. Reveal authority to recover the private working index.
5. THE BOX THAT KEPT BOTH: Morse slate in an actual local recording identifies the index; independently inspect its catalogue marks and the mechanical case's lever diagram. Combine them to release a source card, not a magically encrypted murder confession. Recover the original room recording and a transport-case discrepancy. Never print the complete mechanism solution in a prerequisite document.
6. ACCOUNTS OF THE NIGHT: evidence-based interviews and contradiction testing. Resolve Adrian's and Victor's material omissions through independent records. Julia is still an emotionally believable person. Broader circulation plan adds an available route, not proof of an intruder. Object/label/custody comparisons locate the recovered bookend.
7. WHAT THE ROOM CAN SAY: original prepared-room photo, physical recovered object and its label layering, clinical injury findings, complete reassurance and positive custody/visit records combine. Contact impressions alone do not date contact. Automatic delivery ceases to be a late-life alibi. Julia's trusted role and account are tested with sufficient accessible evidence before any final confrontation.
8. A CASE, NOT A STORY: player supports consequential assertions with independent source groups and rejects tempting overclaims. Personal theories remain separate. Evidence need not be obtained by winning someone's affection; written alternatives preserve factual closure. Publication/privacy/referral choices have understandable consequences.
9. THE EMPTY CHAIR: evidence-based confrontation, time for acknowledgement and emotional reactions, then aftermath. Iris left the recorder running while preparing her own difficult apology, not as a contrived posthumous trap. The complete original and the comforting listening copy can both exist when described honestly and heard with consent. Final factual closure does not depend on publishing the family recording. The moved sofa and additional chair acquire their final, ordinary human meaning.
