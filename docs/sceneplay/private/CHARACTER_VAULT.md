## Dialogue rules

Start with what the person is doing and wants now. Let a source-specific question create pressure. The answer may contain a correction, admission, omission or refusal, but it must not grant knowledge the character lacks. A humane approach can change the route through a conversation; it must not be the sole way to obtain a fact needed for closure.

Ordinary phone material is allowed to be ordinary. Three incomplete lines in the opening belong to real relationship conflicts: Mara's request not to perform reassurance, her resentment about smiling for everyone, and Adrian's objection to a mutually agreed separation account. They should initially raise questions. Their later contexts are documented in the matrix; none becomes an authenticated threat or an alibi merely because the player selects it.
