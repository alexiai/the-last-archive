# THE LAST ARCHIVE — scene-led opening

Open https://alexiai.github.io/the-last-archive/editions/sceneplay-preview/ in your browser. This fresh address avoids an older edition already cached at the main preview address. The same new game also lives at https://alexiai.github.io/the-last-archive/preview/ . Both addresses share this new edition’s save.

Choose **New game**. A voiced animated introduction leads directly into the apartment. Drag to look around; tap objects or the floor to approach them. The **Move** control is a navigation assist. On a keyboard, use arrow keys to look and E to interact with the centre of the view.

Examine objects in the room. During a close examination, drag to lean around the detail, use Lean closer / Ease back and keep observations you want to return to. Recover the authorised phone copy from the side table. Its conversations, photographs, unsent attachment and voice note are available through the device itself. Photographs support panning, pinch/slider magnification, source marks, original capture information and equivalent descriptions.

Return to the officer when you have a comparison to explain. Optional hints are available in conversation. Keeping an observation does not automatically confirm a finding.

The notebook is an optional carried tool, not the main navigation screen. Private notes and observations survive reload. **Pause** has sound, reduced motion, replay, export/import and a link to the preserved previous edition. Back up a save before clearing browser data or changing device. Saves do not synchronize between devices.

This is one complete opening section, not the complete rebuilt campaign. It ends at the next enquiry; you can return to the apartment and keep investigating. No several-hour duration has been independently measured. Physical iPhone performance and independent puzzle/narrative testing remain necessary.

The previous casework edition remains at https://alexiai.github.io/the-last-archive/editions/casework-preview/ with its existing `lastarchive.casework.v1` save. The new section uses `lastarchive.sceneplay.v1`. Earlier production editions retain their original saves. No migration or reset is applied to them.

Once the first online installation completes, the scene, sound and film can be used offline. This game needs a WebGL 2-capable browser. It needs no installation, Python, local server, account, paid service or runtime AI.
